SUMMARY: Three SQL injection vulnerabilities were confirmed across the DVWA application. Two are error-based injection points in the main SQLi and brute-force modules, and one is a boolean-blind injection in the blind SQLi module. All three allow unauthenticated attackers to manipulate database queries.
